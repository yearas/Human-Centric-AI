Project 4 - Preference elicitation

This study investigates how a recommender system can learn your personal movie taste from a small number of choices. You will be shown movies from the IMDB 5000 dataset and asked which of them you prefer.

No personal data is collected. Only your choices and the time you needed for them are stored, under a number that is not linked to your identity. You can stop at any time by simply closing this page.
